How do I back up and restore my data?

Open Settings › Backup. Connect Google Drive for encrypted automatic backups, or create a backup file and save it in Files or iCloud Drive. To restore, choose the backup in the same screen.

I got a new iPhone. How do I move my data?

Back up on your old iPhone and make sure iCloud Keychain (Passwords) is on for your Apple Account. Install AleeFin on the new iPhone, signed in to the same Apple Account, then restore from Google Drive or your backup file in Settings › Backup.

How do I cancel AleeFin Pro?

On your iPhone open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › AleeFin, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Your records stay available on the Free plan.

I bought Pro but it isn't active.

Open AleeFin Pro in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

Does AleeFin connect to my bank?

No. AleeFin never asks for bank logins. With Pro you can import CSV statements downloaded from your bank and approve each transaction.

How do I delete my data?

Delete records in the app, or delete AleeFin to remove all data from your iPhone. Delete any backup from your Google Drive or wherever you saved it.

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026.

Your financial data stays on your iPhone. Accounts, transactions, loans, assets, budgets, goals, receipts and notes are stored on your device and protected by iOS encryption while your iPhone is locked. AleeFin has no server or database that holds them. We never see, sell or share your financial data.

Backups you choose. If you connect Google Drive, AleeFin asks only for access to the files it creates, and uploads an encrypted backup to your own Drive. The encryption key is kept in your Keychain and synced by iCloud Keychain, which Apple end-to-end encrypts, so your backup also opens on your other iPhones with the same Apple Account. You can also save a backup file wherever you choose, such as the Files app or iCloud Drive. We have no access to either.

Anonymous usage and crash reports. AleeFin uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to learn which features are used and to fix crashes. These reports include events such as opening a feature, completing a backup or starting a subscription, crash logs, device model, iOS version, a random app-installation identifier and a general location (country or city) that Google estimates from your IP address. They never include amounts, account names, transactions or other financial details, are not linked to your identity, and are not used for advertising.

Live prices. To show exchange rates, gold and market prices, AleeFin requests public data from open.er-api.com, gold-api.com, Yahoo Finance and CoinGecko. These requests contain only currency codes or the ticker symbols you track, never your balances or personal details.

Face ID and notifications. Face ID is handled by iOS; AleeFin never receives biometric data. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.

Purchases. AleeFin Pro is sold through Apple. We receive only whether your subscription is active, never your payment details.

No ads, no tracking, no data sales. AleeFin shows no ads, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell data.

Your choices. Delete a record in the app, or delete the app to remove all data on your iPhone. Delete your backup from Google Drive, or disconnect Google Drive in Settings, at any time.

Children. AleeFin is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact. We will post any changes to this policy in the app and on our website. Questions: aleemktm@icloud.com
